\subsection{Optimization effects on the generated group}
The per-instance table (post-reset-completion input, optimized automaton, export, symbolic grouping and stage times for the 29 completed inputs), the aggregate table and the transition-count figure are in Supplement Section~\ref{S-sec:generated}. The largest effect is R6: Spot emits 64 states and 15,625 cube-split transitions; six rounds leave 640 edges and six clocks, and export then produces 1,837 explicit edges, 63 invariant-bearing locations and 3,360 clock-difference guard occurrences (448 symbolic transitions). The larger explicit count is the cost of the target representation, not a reversal of the optimization. The optimizer's cost depends on its input relation, and the upstream producer can dominate total time (N8 spends most of its time in Spot); these cases support no general runtime or size bound. The runs of this subsection used the former default, Spot level \texttt{small}; with level \texttt{low} the total over the 29 configurations falls from 152 s to 58 s (one run against the five-run medians) with identical optimized sizes except for F5 and F6 (25 to 27 symbolic transitions). The optimizer, export and initial-value check were since made faster by verified rewritings (Section~\ref{sec:speedups}), so the stage timings are those of the earlier implementation.
